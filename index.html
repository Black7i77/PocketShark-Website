HOW IT WORKS

Three steps. No root shell.

PocketShark uses Android's approved local VPN path to observe traffic from the phone while keeping the workflow understandable.

  1. 01

    Start capture

    Approve Android's standard VPN consent screen and start a local capture session.

  2. 02

    Inspect traffic

    Filter by protocol, endpoint, port, or direction and drill into packet-level details.

  3. 03

    Review or export

    Use built-in statistics or export a PCAP for deeper analysis in desktop Wireshark.

CLEAR AND HONEST LIMITS

Android boundaries, explained.

PocketShark is intentionally transparent about what an ordinary Android app can and cannot inspect.

01

Phone traffic only. PocketShark does not provide laptop-style promiscuous Wi-Fi capture of every device on the network.

02

Live IPv4. In v0.2.0, IPv6 bypasses PocketShark and continues normally.

03

Encryption stays encryption. TLS and QUIC traffic stays encrypted; PocketShark does not decrypt protected sessions.

04

One VPN at a time. Android permits one VPN service per user/profile, so starting capture disconnects another active VPN.

OBSERVATION, NOT ATTACK

Built to inspect — not interfere.

No packet injection, spoofing, or traffic modification. Use PocketShark only with owned or authorised devices, networks, and captures.
